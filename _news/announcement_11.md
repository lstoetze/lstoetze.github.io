New publication with [Konstantin Bogatyrev](https://sites.google.com/view/konstantin-bogatyrev/) in [Political Analysis](https://doi.org/10.1017/pan.2026.10046) on [Estimating Treatment Effects on Proportions with Synthetic Controls](https://doi.org/10.1017/pan.2026.10046). Conventional synthetic control methods are built for a single outcome, forcing researchers to model each share of a compositional outcome separately. We propose jointly estimating synthetic controls across all shares using the same weights, giving a constant control comparison and interpretable treatment effect estimates for compositional panel data.
